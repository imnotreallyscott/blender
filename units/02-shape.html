Unit 2: Shape things

Move, change, and build shapes from simple blocks. Each lesson ends with a small project.

  • 2Move, rotate, scale

    The three tools you'll use for almost everything. Project: a block stand.

  • 3Edit mode and vertices

    Change a shape, not just its position. Project: a shield plate.

  • 4Extrude

    Pull a flat shape into 3D. Project: a raised name tag.

  • 5Bevel and smooth

    Round hard edges so models look finished. Project: a rounded coaster.
