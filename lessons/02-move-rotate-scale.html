Lesson 2: Move, rotate, scale

About 12 minutes · Blender 5.2 LTS · Mac Mini

Goal: Use the three basic transform keys to place, turn, and size objects.

The three keys

  • G = Grab (move)
  • R = Rotate
  • S = Scale

Select an object, press the key, move the mouse, and click to confirm. Press Esc to cancel.

Lock to one direction

After pressing G, R, or S, press X, Y, or Z to lock the change to one axis. Press the same letter again to switch to the object's own axis. This is the most useful habit in Blender.

Steps

  1. Select one cube. Press G, then Z, move the mouse up, and click. The cube rises.
  2. Press Cmd + Z to undo. Repeat with G then X.
  3. Press R, then Z, and move the mouse. Watch the cube turn. Hold Ctrl while rotating to snap to set angles (check that this works on your Mac; unverified on your trackpad).
  4. Press S and move the mouse to make the cube bigger or smaller. Press S, then X, to stretch it only along X.
  5. Look at the N panel. Press N with the mouse in the 3D Viewport. It shows exact Location, Rotation, and Scale numbers. Type a number to set a value precisely.
Why this matters: Blender measures in units, and the N panel shows the numbers. If something looks off, check the numbers before you guess.

Project: three-block bison-shaped stand

This is a simple stand with a wide base and a shorter stacked top. It is not a bison yet. It is a shape, and it teaches the three tools.

  1. Use the cube you have. Press S, then X, and stretch it into a wide, flat base.
  2. Duplicate one cube with Shift + D, right-click to drop it, then press S to make it smaller. This is the body.
  3. Duplicate once more for the head. Scale it to a small block, then use G, Z to lift it above the body.
  4. Use G with X or Y to line up the parts. Save with Cmd + S.
