Lesson 6: Render a still image

About 12 minutes · Blender 5.2.1 LTS · Mac Mini + Magic Mouse

What rendering does

A render takes the view from a camera, adds light, and makes a finished picture. The 3D view you have been working in is a workspace. A render is the result you can share.

Steps

  1. Tool Open your block stand file from Lesson 2. If you don't have it, use any simple object.
  2. Key Press 0 (top row, with Emulate Numpad on from Lesson 0) to look through the camera. unverified on your Mac The view should show your objects. If not, use the camera tools in the 3D View later.
  3. Menu In the Properties panel, click the Render tab. unverified icon position for 5.2.1
  4. Menu Find Render Engine and choose EEVEE. EEVEE is the fast option for a Mac Mini. unverified default on 5.2.1
  5. Menu In the top bar, choose Render > Render Image. A new window opens and fills in. unverified menu name
  6. Check The picture shows your objects with light and shadow.
  7. Menu In the render window, choose Image > Save As. Pick PNG, name it stand-render.png, and save it. unverified menu name
If the render looks dark or empty: the camera may be pointed at nothing. Move your objects into the middle of the view, then render again.

Project: your first render

Render your block stand and save it as a PNG. Keep the file. You will render it again after Lesson 9, so you can see the difference color makes.
