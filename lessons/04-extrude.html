Lesson 4: Extrude

About 14 minutes · Blender 5.2 LTS · Mac Mini

Goal: Pull a flat face out into a solid shape using E, the extrude key.

What extrude does

Extrude copies a selected face (or edge) and pulls the copy away from the surface, creating new side walls. It is the most-used modeling tool in Blender. Press E in Edit Mode, move the mouse, and click to confirm.

Steps

  1. Select the default cube and press Tab to enter Edit Mode.
  2. Press 3 for face select. Click the top face so it turns orange.
  3. Press E, move the mouse up, and click. The top is now a raised block.
  4. Press Cmd + Z to undo. Press I to inset: a smaller face appears inside the first. Move the mouse and click.
  5. Press E again to extrude the inset face. You now have a raised frame.
  6. Press Tab to leave Edit Mode.
Repeat rule: Pressing Shift + R repeats your last action. It is a fast way to build stepped shapes.

Project: raised name tag

This tag is original. You will use the blue and gold colors later, in Lesson 5.

  1. Add a cube, then press S, X, 2, Enter to make it wider. Press S, Y, 0.5, Enter to make it a flat plate.
  2. Enter Edit Mode with Tab. Select the top face with 3 and click it.
  3. Press I, move the mouse a little, and click to inset a border.
  4. Press E and move the mouse up a little to raise the middle panel. Press Tab to exit Edit Mode.
  5. Save with Cmd + S as nametag.blend.
