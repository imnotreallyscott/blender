Lesson 4: Extrude

About 14 minutes · Blender 5.2.1 LTS · Mac Mini + Magic Mouse

What extrude does

Extrude copies a selected face and pulls the copy away from the surface. Blender builds new side walls for you. It is the most-used modeling tool.

Steps

  1. Tool Select the cube and press Tab for Edit Mode.
  2. Key Click the face select icon (the last of the three small icons next to Edit Mode).
  3. Mouse Click the top face. It turns orange.
  4. Key Press E, move the mouse up, and click. The top is now a raised block.
  5. Check You see a tall block. The new side walls are visible.
  6. Key Press Cmd + Z to undo.
  7. Key Press I to inset. A smaller face appears inside the first. Move the mouse and click.
  8. Key Press E again and move up. You now have a raised frame.
  9. Key Press Tab to leave Edit Mode.
Repeat trick: press Shift + R to repeat your last action. Great for stepped shapes. unverified on your Mac keyboard

Project: raised name tag

A flat plate with a raised center panel and a border. The colors are for later lessons.

  1. Tool Add a cube: Shift + A, then Mesh > Cube.
  2. Key Press S, X, type 2, Enter to make it wider.
  3. Key Press S, Y, type 0.5, Enter to make it a flat plate.
  4. Key Press Tab, then click the face select icon (the last of the three small icons next to Edit Mode).
  5. Mouse Click the top face.
  6. Key Press I, move the mouse slightly, and click. This makes a border.
  7. Key Press E, move up a little, click. The center panel rises.
  8. Key Press Tab to leave Edit Mode. Save as nametag.blend with Cmd + S.
