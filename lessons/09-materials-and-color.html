Lesson 9: Materials and color

About 14 minutes · Blender 5.2.1 LTS · Mac Mini + Magic Mouse

What a material does

A material controls how a surface looks: its color, shininess, and how light passes through it. Each object can have a material. Materials show up in renders. Your Lesson 6 render will look different once you add them.

Steps

  1. Tool Open the stand file from Lesson 2. Click the base block to select it.
  2. Menu In the Properties panel, click the Material tab. It is the sphere icon, last in the tab column. icon position unverified on 5.2.1
  3. Menu Click New. A material named Material appears in the slot. unverified label on 5.2.1
  4. Mouse Double-click the name and type Base Green, then press Enter.
  5. Menu Under Surface, click the color box next to Base Color. Pick a color in the window that opens. Surface panel and label unverified on 5.2.1
  6. Check The base block changes color in the 3D view.
  7. Key Click the body block, then repeat steps 2 to 5 with a new material and a different color. Do the same for the head block.
  8. Menu Choose Render > Render Image again, the same way as Lesson 6. Save it as stand-painted.png.
Why each block has its own material: in Lesson 2, the base, body, and head are three separate objects. Each object takes one material, so this is the easiest way to paint them now. Painting different parts of a single object is a later lesson.
