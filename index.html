Blender Basics · scottaferguson.org

Learn Blender, one short lesson at a time

Written for Blender 5.2 LTS on a Mac Mini. Each lesson takes about 10 to 15 minutes and ends with a small project you can finish and see.

How to use this site
  1. Open Blender and keep this page in a second window.
  2. Do one lesson. Stop when the timer or your energy runs out.
  3. Tick the boxes at the end. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Lessons

  • Lesson 1: Find your way around
    Move the view, pick things, save your file. Project: a stacked "716" cube block.
  • Lesson 2: Move, rotate, scale
    The three tools you will use for almost everything. Project: a three-block bison-shaped stand.
  • Lesson 3: Edit mode and vertices
    Change the shape of an object, not just its position. Project: a tilted shield plate.
  • Lesson 4: Extrude
    Pull a flat shape into 3D. Project: a raised Buffalo-blue name tag.
  • Lesson 5: Bevel and smooth
    Round the hard edges so models look finished. Project: a rounded coaster.
